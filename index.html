02 · How it works

Set the schedule. Walk away.

  1. 01

    Sees the field

    A Jetson Orin and a depth camera detect the field visually, in real time. No GPS, no beacons, nothing buried in the dirt.

  2. 02

    Chalks every line

    A precision chalk system lays down baselines, foul lines, and batter’s boxes — straight, square, every time.

  3. 03

    Drags every inch

    The folding drag arm levels the infield, auto-lifts over the mound, and folds flat for transport between fields.

  4. 04

    Runs from your phone

    Pick baseball or softball, schedule Field 1 for 6:00 AM and Field 2 for 6:20, hit start, and walk away. The robot sends a notification as each field wraps.
