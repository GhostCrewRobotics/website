The System

One robot. Every line, every pass, every field.

Edge vision, a precision chalk system, and a self-lifting drag arm, all run from one app. Nothing gets buried in your dirt.

  1. 01 / VISION

    Jetson Edge Vision & Depth Sensing

    Sub-centimeter infield visual segmentation without GPS or buried wires. The robot sees the field in real time and plans every pass itself.
